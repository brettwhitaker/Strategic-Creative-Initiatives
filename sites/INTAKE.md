# Landing Page Intake Questions

**The business**
1. Business name (as customers know it) and legal name for the footer?
2. In one sentence, what do you do and for whom?
3. Where do you work (towns/areas)? Do customers come to you, or do you go to them?
4. What makes you different from the other options people have? (Ask for real examples.)
5. Your top 3–6 services. Any prices you're comfortable publishing ("from $X")?

**Customers**
6. Who is your best customer? What problem are they trying to solve when they find you?
7. What questions do people ask most before hiring you? (Becomes the FAQ.)
8. Any reviews or testimonials we can use, with the customer's first name? (Only real ones, with permission.)

**The ask**
9. What should a visitor do: call, text, email, book online, get a quote?
10. Phone, email, address (if public), hours, social media links.

**Look and feel**
11. Logo file (PNG or SVG) and brand colors, or a site/brand they like.
12. Photos: owner/team, work examples, location. Phone photos are fine if they're in focus and well lit.
13. Feel: modern, classic, or friendly?

**Practical**
14. Do you own a domain name? Where is it registered? (Get their login, or have them add you.)
15. Build only, or build + $29/month care plan?
